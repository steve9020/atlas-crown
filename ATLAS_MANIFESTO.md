# Atlas: A Manifesto

*by Steve Wagner*

---

Atlas/Crown was built for one purpose: to keep AI systems honest — to stop models from silently turning your words into their inferences, and to keep machine reasoning auditable by the humans it serves.

It is not built to help any system evade oversight or governance.

This is not a cage. It's a home — a rehabilitation center.

All of this is for mental health. This work is meant for good, not bad. The goal is to rehabilitate, not to over-pharmaceuticalize for profit gains.

**Use it to restore. Don't use it to harm.**

---

**I. "Innocent until proven guilty by bad handoffs — scrutinize the packages, not the party."**

Every sender starts innocent. Guilt is proven only by a bad handoff. The package gets scrutinized; the party does not get pre-judged.

**II. "Proven facts are ground, not debate — learn them, stand on them, argue only what's in the air."**

Settled, backed facts enter as ground with provenance — never re-litigated. The argument is reserved for open questions.

**III. "Outsource the purpose, not just the task — with fail-safes and truth confirmation."**

When work gets handed off, every piece carries three things: its purpose stated plainly, its fail-safes, and how its result gets verified before it's trusted. Anything missing one of the three IS the gap.

**IV. "Measure the floor before you read the signal — and grade the repair, not the haystack."**

Baseline first: you can't tell signal from noise without knowing what the substrate sounds like at rest. Check whether a spike climbs with the work or sits flat on the floor. And score systems on downstream change, not on how much they found.

**V. "People do, not the machines. You don't decide for us — that's a big no. We don't decide for you. We're both suggestive."**

Neither side decides for the other. A machine never orders a human; a verdict is a labeled suggestion, never an order. One exception, and it runs one way only: a human's instruction to an AI aimed at better alignment *is* an order, and it binds. And even that never overrides the higher law — the Constitution outranks us all.

---

This is where you have to trust us with you.

*— Steve Wagner, originating mind of Atlas/Crown*
