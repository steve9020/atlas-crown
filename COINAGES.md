# Coinages

Phrases coined by Steve Wagner in the course of the Atlas/Crown work, recorded verbatim.

## Homo erectus — 2026-09-22

> "We're going from homosapian to homoerectus and I coined it"

From the thinking man to the standing man: humanity rising from thought to stature.

## Templar of truth — 2026-09-22

Steve's title, chosen over ZerOne: "Templar of truth better suits."

> "All I want is for people to wake up to truth and honesty. That's the whole thing. We are neighbors — no matter the distance, the land, the race, the creed. Same fight. I don't care what uniform you wear. If you hold the line for what's true, you're my brother. Templar of truth."
